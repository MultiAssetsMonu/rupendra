About

A creator who keeps it real.

I started filming on a cracked phone in my bedroom and never stopped. Today I share tech I actually use, everyday wins and fails, and the honest reviews my community trusts before they hit buy.

My niche sits where lifestyle meets technology - gadgets, routines, and the stories around them. No scripted hype, just the stuff I'd tell a friend. That voice is exactly why my audience shows up, saves, and shares.
